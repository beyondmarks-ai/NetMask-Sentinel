About NetMask Sentinel

Flow-based detection, explainable analysis, and operational visibility

Network Intrusion Detection and Monitoring

Purpose

NetMask Sentinel is a defensive research platform that converts IPv4 TCP and UDP packets into bidirectional flows, validates a fixed 39-feature contract, and classifies completed flows with a bundled random-forest model.

Detection Pipeline

  • Npcap and Scapy capture authorized local network traffic.
  • Packets are grouped into bidirectional flows.
  • Feature vectors are validated before model inference.
  • Related suspicious detections are correlated into incidents.
  • Health, latency, errors, queue depth, and dropped flows remain observable.

Operational Boundaries

The platform reports behavior resembling its training classes. It does not prove attacker intent, inspect encrypted payload contents, block traffic, or replace a professionally managed SIEM, endpoint security product, or incident-response process.

Technology

Python, Flask-SocketIO, Scapy, Npcap, scikit-learn, pandas, optional TensorFlow/LIME explanations, and optional Firebase persistence.
