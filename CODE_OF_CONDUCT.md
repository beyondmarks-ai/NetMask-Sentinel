# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces must remain respectful, professional, inclusive, and focused on defensive security engineering. Constructive disagreement is welcome; harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized content, deliberate disruption, and unauthorized offensive-security activity are not.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical decisions with evidence and empathy.
- Respect privacy, licensing, disclosure timelines, and authorization boundaries.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes promptly.
- Keep demonstrations bounded to systems the participant owns or is authorized to test.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens people, systems, privacy, or the project community. Security-sensitive reports must follow `SECURITY.md` rather than public discussion.
